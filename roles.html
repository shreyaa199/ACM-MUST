Roles & Responsibilities

🧭 Your Impact

Every member plays a vital role in our collective success.

👩‍💻

General Member

Participate in workshops, contribute ideas in meetings, and represent the chapter in university events.

👑

Leadership

Lead sub-committees, mentor juniors, and help in strategic planning for the chapter's growth.

🤝

Event Volunteer

Assist in organizing hackathons, speaker sessions, and maintaining discipline during on-campus activities.

🎨

Creative & Tech

Manage social media, design posters, or maintain the chapter's digital presence and website.

📢

Public Relations

Collaborate with other chapters, handle outreach, and ensure the chapter's news reaches everyone.

📝

Documentation

Maintain records of all events, write reports, and handle formal correspondence and meeting minutes.
