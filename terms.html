Guidelines to ensure a professional and ethical environment.

Membership Terms

1. Membership is non-transferable and valid for the current academic session.

2. Attendance in at least 75% of mandatory meetings is required to retain active status.

3. Misuse of the ACM-W logo or name for personal projects without permission is strictly prohibited.

4. Any form of intellectual property theft during hackathons will lead to immediate expulsion.

5. Members must maintain respectful and inclusive behavior.

6. The core team reserves the right to modify these terms with prior notice.

7. Members must uphold the reputation of MUST ACM-W.

8. Violation may result in suspension or removal.
